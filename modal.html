DIFFERENT TYPES OF RICE AND MODE OF PREPARATIONS

ORIGIN

white rice is a traditional food that was eaten across nigeria and was the foundation to all the
other major rice dishes in nigeria

    METHOD OF PREPARATION

    List of ingredients:
  1. Rice
  2. Tomatoes
  3. pepper
  4. onions
  5. salt
  6. cube seasoning
  7. fish/meat (optional)

    How to cook:

  1. First put 3cups of rice in a bowl and wash with water and salt to remove starch
  2. Put the washed rice in a pot and add salt then let it boil for about 15-20mins
  3. Drain the rice with a sieve and set aside
  4. wash the tomatoes the pepper and the onions then put in a blender and blend until smooth
  5. In another pot put in some oil and let it heat up
  6. then put in the blended mix add salt, seasoning and the fried fish and let it fry for about 10mins.
  7. Serve on a flat plate with any type of drink to your like

ORIGIN

Jollof rice is a common west african food accross west africa with each countries
like nigeria having their own preferred method of preparation.

    METHOD OF PREPARATION

    List of ingredients:
  1. Rice
  2. Tomatoes&Tin tomato
  3. pepper
  4. onions
  5. salt
  6. cube seasoning
  7. jollof spice
  8. fish/meat (optional)

    How to cook:

  1. First, put the cups of rice in a bowl and wash with water and salt to remove starch
  2. wash the tomatoes the pepper and the onions then put in a blender and blend until smooth
  3. Add oil into a pan and let it heat up before putting some chopped onions and saute it for 3mins
  4. When onions is almost brown add the tin tomato and continously stir for 5mins
  5. Add the blended mix into the pot, add salt and the seasonings then fry till almost done.
  6. After add the washed rice and add water then let it cook for about 30mins
  7. Serve on a flat plate with any type of drink to your like
  8. You can also serve with meat or fish depending on your taste

ORIGIN

Fried rice is a popular rice dish with different methods and on how to make it. it involves the use of different vegies to make it look more appealing.

    METHOD OF PREPARATION

    List of ingredients:
  1. Rice
  2. carrot, peas,greenbeans,bell peppers.
  3. pepper
  4. onions
  5. salt
  6. cube seasoning
  7. fried rice spice
  8. fish/meat/chicken
  9. tumeric

    How to cook:

  1. dice all the vegies listed into small cube sizes and set aside
  2. wash the meat thoroughly and boil in a pot till well cooked then seperate the stock from the meat
  3. add oil into a pot and fry some chopped onions and pepper
  4. wash the rice and add it to the pot then let if fry for 3mins
  5. Pour in the meat stock and all the seasonings stir and let it cook for about 25-30mins
  6. In another pan put little oil and pour in the vegies with a pinch of salt and fry until soften
  7. Add the vegies into the rice when almost done and combine well
  8. Serve on a flat plate with any type of drink to your like
  9. You can also serve with meat/fish/chicken depending on your taste
